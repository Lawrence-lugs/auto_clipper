Note

Score is the segment’s mean messages/minute minus the stream’s median rate. It rewards intensity, not length; the range, duration, and message count are reported alongside it so the ranking can be re-interpreted.

Boundary effect: the smoothed curve tapers at the stream edges, while segment means use raw counts. The end-of-stream goodbye burst therefore has a higher mean (79/min) than the smoothed line suggests there (~54/min).

Method at a glance

  1. Download the live_chat replay with yt-dlp and parse video offsets.
  2. Drop waiting-room messages collapsed onto t=0; deduplicate replay batches.
  3. Count messages in 10-second bins and smooth with a 90-second Gaussian.
  4. Detect threshold crossings with hysteresis, merge short gaps, split long runs, and drop blips.
  5. Rank segments by mean rate above the baseline; export CSV, JSON, and a shaded chart.

The full algorithm, parameter table, related work, and limitations are in the segmentation note.

Caveats

  • Chat replay must exist and may omit deleted/filtered messages.
  • Message counts are not unique viewers; one excited viewer can post many times.
  • Start/end greetings and goodbyes are structural bursts, not necessarily clip candidates.
  • The threshold is calibrated on this stream. Use the auto threshold or tune --threshold per channel.
